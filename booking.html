The Process

1

Inquiry & Vision

Submit your initial details. We review your date, venue, and aesthetic inspirations to ensure we are the perfect fit.

2

The Consultation

A 45-minute collaborative discussion to dive deep into your color palette, preferred textures, and the overall feeling you wish to evoke.

3

Proposal & Design Deck

We present a customized, visual mood board and detailed preliminary estimate outlining the botanical ingredients and installations.

4

Securing Your Date

Upon approval of the proposal, a signed contract and retainer officially reserve our studio for your celebration.
